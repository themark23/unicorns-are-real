Unicorn Valley is a magical play world for kids ages 4–11, designed to spark imagination one sparkle at a time.

Meet the Unicorn Valley family — Mama Celestia, Papa Orion, Daughter Lumi, Son Comet, and Baby Twinkle — five enchanting unicorn characters, each with their own colour, personality, and wiggly idle animation. Kids drag them freely across six breathtaking hand-painted panoramic worlds: Sparkle Beach, Magic Garden, Rainbow Space, Crystal Forest, Fairy Park, and Star Castle. Each scene is packed with 10 hidden magical items to discover, from starfish wands and coral crowns on the beach to shimmering gem crystals in the forest and sparkling treasures inside the castle. Touch an item and watch it light up with glitter and sparkles. Put it near a character and something magical happens.

There are no rules, no scores, no timers, no ads, and no accounts. Just a child, five unicorn friends, and a world full of wonder to explore at their own pace.

Website FAQ
What is Unicorn Valley?
Unicorn Valley is a free-play app for children ages 4 to 11. Kids drag five unicorn characters — Mama Celestia, Papa Orion, Daughter Lumi, Son Comet, and Baby Twinkle — across six magical scenes and discover sparkly hidden items along the way. There are no levels, no goals, and no wrong answers. It's pure imaginative play.

How do you play?
Just press and drag any unicorn to move them around the scene. Tap a glittery item (seashells, wands, crystals, treats, and more) to make it sparkle. Drag a unicorn close to an item and watch the magic happen. Swipe the scene left or right to explore the full panoramic world. Tap any scene thumbnail at the bottom to travel to a new world instantly.

What are the six worlds?
Sparkle Beach, Magic Garden, Rainbow Space, Crystal Forest, Fairy Park, and Star Castle — each with its own painted landscape, colour palette, background music, and 10 unique items to find and play with.

Is there any reading required?
No. The entire app is played by touch — drag, tap, and explore. There's no text, no instructions, and nothing to read, making it perfectly accessible for pre-readers.

Is it safe for young children?
Yes. Unicorn Valley collects zero personal data, has no ads, no in-app purchases, no social features, no external links, and no internet connection required after download. It is completely self-contained.

Does it work without WiFi?
Yes. Once downloaded, the app works entirely offline — no internet required.

Are there in-app purchases or subscriptions?
No. Unicorn Valley is a one-time download with no upsells, no subscriptions, and no hidden costs of any kind.

What devices does it run on?
Unicorn Valley is designed for iPhone. It runs on any iPhone with iOS 16 or later.

Will there be more scenes or characters?
We're just getting started! Future updates are planned with new worlds, seasonal scenes, and more magical surprises. Follow us to stay in the loop.
