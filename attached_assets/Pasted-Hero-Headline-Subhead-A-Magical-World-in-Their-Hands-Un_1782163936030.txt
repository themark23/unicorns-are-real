App Store Description
Unicorn Magic — Sparkle, Color & Play

Step into a world of rainbows, potions, and magical friends. Unicorn Magic is an all-in-one creative play app built just for little ones — packed with five activities that kids can explore on their own, at their own pace.

✨ Dress Your Unicorn
Customize your very own unicorn with color zones, cutie marks, and accessories like crowns, tiaras, and bow ties. Toggle on Sparkle Mode for extra magic. Every combination is uniquely yours.

📖 Read & Listen
Choose from 15 original stories and 15 fun unicorn facts — all written with kindness and wonder in mind. Tap "Read This Story Aloud" and let the app narrate the adventure while your child follows along.

🎨 Color & Create
Pick a color, pick a scene, and tap to fill. Ten gorgeous unicorn coloring pages come to life with a single touch. Kids can save their finished art or clear it and start over as many times as they like.

🧪 Brew Magic Potions
Ten secret potion recipes are waiting to be discovered. Tap each ingredient to watch it drop into the bottle, see the color transform, and reveal what the potion does. A little science. A lot of magic.

🎮 Play & Win
Challenge your memory in a unicorn-themed Memory Match game or go head-to-head in Tic-Tac-Toe. Perfect for solo play or a quick game with a friend.

Made for ages 4–8 · Ad-free · No in-app purchases · Works offline
